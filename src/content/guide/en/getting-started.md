Trasio is a trip planner. It helps you plan a trip for one day or more,
with notes, places along the way and transport there and back. It remembers
the places you want to visit and where you have been. And it finds journeys
with stops you can walk to.

Trasio works only in the Czech Republic. The map, public transport journeys
and places cover only Czechia. You cannot plan a trip abroad in it.

The app is in Czech for now. This guide uses the Czech names of buttons and
screens, with a translation.

## Where to find Trasio

- **Web:** [app.gotrasio.cz](https://app.gotrasio.cz) — works on a computer and on a phone.
- **Android:** the Android app. It also works without signal.

## Without an account

Without an account you can:

- search for journeys,
- look at the map, stops and places,
- open a trip that someone sent you as a link.

## With an account

After you sign in you can save plans and trips, upload tracks, rate places
and share trips. Sign in with **Přihlásit se** (Sign in) at the bottom left
(on a phone in the bottom bar).

## Account level and trip limit

The number of saved trips is limited. This protects the service from
abuse; it is not a paid feature. Every account has a level:

| Level | Saved trips | Mapy.cz base map |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 20 | no |
| **Pro** | 1,000 | no |
| **Unlimited** | no limit | yes |

A new account starts at Free. The operator sets a higher level for each
account one by one. You can see how many trips you have saved and your
level in **Profil** (Profile) under **Úroveň účtu** (Account level).

When you reach the limit, you cannot save a new trip. Delete some old
trips, or ask for a higher level. The limits can change over time.

## Main parts of the app

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Mapa** (Map) | Your trips, places and stops on the map. New trips start here. |
| **Výlety** (Trips) | List of trips: New, Walked, Archive. |
| **Místa** (Places) | List of places with ratings, and the places you visited. |
| **Spojení** (Journeys) | Public transport journey search. |
| **Profil** (Profile) | Settings, home point, preferred trip day. |
