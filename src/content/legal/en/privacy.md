## 2. Which data we process

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail, name, sign-in details | Account and sign-in | Contract |
| Plans, trips, points, notes, tags | Service features | Contract |
| Track records (GPX, KML), trip date | Trip archive, statistics | Contract |
| Visited places, ratings, notes on places | Service features | Contract |
| Home point, profile settings | Prefilled search | Contract |
| Technical logs (IP address, time, errors) | Operation and security | Legitimate interest |

Journey search works without an account. We do not link the searched places
to you.

## 3. Location and route privacy

- Track records and trips are **private** by default.
- The option **Hide start and end** hides the first and the last kilometre
  of the route from others.
- Nobody but you sees the state of your trip (new, active, walked).
- Only the average place rating is public, not your rating.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account data and content: while the account exists.
- After you close the account we delete data within 30 days, backups
  within 90 days.
- Technical logs: 14 days.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict
processing, to data portability (trip export to GPX) and to object. You can
complain to the [Office for Personal Data Protection](https://uoou.gov.cz/en).
