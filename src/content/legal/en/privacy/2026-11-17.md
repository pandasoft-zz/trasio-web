## 1. Controller

The controller of personal data is the operator of the service, a private
person.

Controller: **Martin Vídeňský**, contact:
[support@pandasoft.cz](mailto:support@pandasoft.cz).

## 2. Which data we process

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail, name, sign-in details | Account and sign-in | Contract |
| Plans, trips, points, notes, tags | Service features | Contract |
| Track records (GPX, KML), trip date | Trip archive, statistics | Contract |
| Visited places, ratings, notes on places | Service features | Contract |
| Saved places (My places), profile settings | Prefilled search | Contract |
| Photos and their captions (a photo can also show other people) | Photos of places and trips | Contract |
| Acceptance of the terms (version, time, app) | Proof of the consent to the terms | Contract, legitimate interest |
| Technical logs (IP address, time, errors) | Operation and security | Legitimate interest |

Journey search works without an account. We do not link the searched places
to you.

## 3. Location and route privacy

- Track records and trips are **private** by default.
- The option **Hide start and end** hides the first and the last kilometre
  of the route from others.
- Nobody but you sees the state of your trip (new, active, walked).
- Only the average place rating is public, not your rating.
- When you upload a photo, we remove all its metadata, including the GPS
  position, the time and the camera data. Photos of private trips and
  places are seen only by people who may see the trip or the place. Your
  name is shown with a photo only if you choose it in **Profil › Účet**
  (Profile › Account).

## 4. Who processes data for us

| Processor | Purpose | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Contabo GmbH | Server, database and e-mail of the service | EU |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Website delivery, attack protection | EU / USA (standard contractual clauses) |
| Seznam.cz, a.s. (Mapy.cz API) | Walking and cycling routes — gets only point coordinates, no data about you | Czechia |

**AI assistant (MCP).** When you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude
or ChatGPT) to Trasio, Trasio gives it your trips, plans and profile on your
instruction. The assistant's provider then processes these data under its
own terms. It is not our processor; you connect and disconnect the connector
yourself.

## 5. How long we keep data

The periods below are the longest possible and they are binding. We
usually delete data sooner.

- Account data and content: while the account exists.
- After you close the account we delete data within 30 days at the latest,
  backups within 90 days at the latest.
- Technical logs: 14 days at the most.
- Your suggestions of public places stay in the shared place database after
  you close the account, without your name.
- A place photo waiting for review: 30 days at the most.
- Approved photos of public places stay published under the open license
  after you close the account, without your name. Other photos are deleted
  with the account.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict
processing, to data portability and to object. You use access, portability
and deletion yourself in **Profil › Moje data** (Profile › My data):

- **Stáhnout ZIP** (Download ZIP) — your trips, places, ratings, visits and
  photos in open formats (GPX, GeoJSON, WebP),
- **Smazat účet natrvalo** (Delete account forever) — deletes the account
  and all your data.

Send other requests to
[support@pandasoft.cz](mailto:support@pandasoft.cz). You can complain to the
[Office for Personal Data Protection](https://uoou.gov.cz/en).
