## 1. Who runs the service

The Trasio service (the website [gotrasio.cz](https://gotrasio.cz), the app
[app.gotrasio.cz](https://app.gotrasio.cz) and the Android app) is run by a
private person as a non-commercial project, not as a business (the
"operator").

Operator: **Martin Vídeňský**, contact:
[support@pandasoft.cz](mailto:support@pandasoft.cz).

## 2. What the service does

Trasio helps you plan trips: it searches public transport journeys, shows a
map and places, and keeps your plans and trips. The whole service is free
and has no paid features.

The service works only in the Czech Republic. The map, public transport
journeys and place data cover only this area.

## 3. Account

- You need an account to save plans and trips.
- The number of saved trips is limited by the account level. This
  protects the service from abuse. The operator sets the level for each
  account one by one and can change the limits. There is no right to a
  higher level. The levels are listed in the
  [guide](/en/guide/getting-started/).
- The account level also sets how many photos you can add to one trip.
- Your account details must be true. Keep your sign-in details safe.
- You can close your account at any time in **Profil › Moje data**
  (Profile › My data). You can download your data there first. We then
  delete your data as the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/) says.

## 4. Timetables and routes are a guide only

We compute journeys from public timetables. A service can be late, cancelled
or changed. Routes and place data come from open data and can be wrong.
**Check important facts with the carrier before you travel.** You are
responsible for your own safety on the route.

## 5. User content

- Plans, trips, notes, ratings and place suggestions are your content.
- You must not create or post content that breaks the law or good morals,
  or that breaks other people's rights.
- The operator can delete such content without notice and block or close
  the account it comes from.
- You allow us to show public or shared content to other users, in the
  scope you set.
- After a review, we can use your place suggestions in the shared place
  database.

- Private trips, trips shared by link, notes, visits and your ratings are

- Add only content that you have the rights to. Do not copy other people's

## 6. Photos

- Upload only photos that you took yourself or that you have the rights
  to. If people can be recognised on a photo, you need their consent.
- Every photo that you upload to a place or a trip is under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
  license. This is also true for photos of private trips and private
  places; but only people who may see the trip or the place see them.
- With every photo we show the license and the author: your name, or
  "Trasio user". You choose it in **Profil › Účet** (Profile › Account).
  The choice is for all your photos, also older ones. The default is
  anonymous.
- We review photos of public places before they are shown. A photo that we
  do not approve in 30 days is deleted. We can refuse a photo, or remove it
  later, without giving a reason.
- The number of photos of a place is limited; the account level sets the
  number of photos of a trip.
- We make a photo smaller (at most 1,600 pixels on the longer side) and
  remove all its metadata, including the GPS position.
- After you close your account, approved photos of public places stay
  published under the open license, without your name. We delete your
  other photos.

## 7. Do not enter confidential information

Trasio is not a store for confidential data. Do not put passwords, ID
document numbers, payment details, sensitive personal data (for example
about health) or other confidential information into plans, trips, notes or
anywhere else. If you do, it is at your own risk.

## 8. Sharing with a link

Anyone with an edit link can change the trip. Send links only to people you
trust. You can cancel a link at any time.

## 9. Availability

## 11. Changes of the terms and consent

- The terms have versions. Every version has the date it is valid from and
